## 🎬 Video Production Tips

1. **Screen Recording**: Use OBS Studio or ScreenFlow to record at 1920×1080 @ 30fps
2. **Music**: Use free, royalty-free music (Epidemic Sound, YouTube Audio Library)
3. **Voiceover**: Record in quiet room with decent microphone
4. **Text Overlays**: Use bold, sans-serif fonts (like your dashboard uses)
5. **Color Grading**: Keep brand colors prominent (dark navy + accent colors)
6. **Duration**: Keep main video to 30–60 seconds; save longer demos for YouTube
