## 🆘 Need Help?

If stuck on any step:

1. **Screenshots not working?**
   - Check VISUAL_ASSETS_IMAGE_GUIDE.md → Screenshots to Capture section
   - Use browser's built-in screenshot tool (Shift+S on Windows)

2. **Copy needs refinement?**
   - Read SOCIAL_COPY_READY_TO_POST.md and adjust for your style
   - Keep it conversational, not salesy

3. **Video too hard?**
   - Use Synthesia.io or Loom (easier than recording manually)
   - Or start with just images + copy (videos are optional)

4. **Not getting bookings?**
   - Refine your email subject lines
   - A/B test different images
   - Call instead of email (higher conversion)

## ✨ Final Tips

1. **Consistency > Perfection** — Post regularly, even if imperfect
2. **Engagement > Reach** — 50 engaged followers beat 5000 silent ones
3. **Test & Learn** — Track what works, double down on it
4. **Personal Touch** — Always personalize cold outreach
5. **Follow Up** — Most people need 5–7 touches before acting
6. **Be Patient** — B2B sales take 2–4 months average
